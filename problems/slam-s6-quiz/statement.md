# Warm-up: Registration

The verbal round before coding: why ICP needs an initial guess, the point-to-point / point-to-plane / GICP / NDT family, how to detect degenerate registration, the Kabsch details, and the lidar odometry design questions.

Conventions: $H = \sum_i \tilde p_i \tilde q_i^\top = U\Sigma V^\top$ for Kabsch. The point-to-plane residual is $r_i = n_i\cdot(Rp_i+t-q_i)$.

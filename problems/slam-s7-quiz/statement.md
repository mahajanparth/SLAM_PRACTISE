# Nonlinear Least Squares: Concepts & Warmups

Rapid-fire questions an interviewer asks before a back-end coding problem. They cover Gauss-Newton vs Levenberg-Marquardt, information matrices, BA sparsity, gauge freedom, robust kernels, and the Schur complement.

Notation: residual $r(x)$, Jacobian $J = \partial r/\partial x$, $H = J^\top \Omega J$, $b = J^\top \Omega r$.
